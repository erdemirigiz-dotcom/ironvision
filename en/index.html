MoyMote

Control your monitor's brightness, blue light and HDR from your phone. MoyMote is a private bridge between your Windows PC, your monitor and your phone that runs on your local network. No account, no cloud.

  • From the couch or the bed: your phone becomes the remote, with nothing to install on it.
  • Brightness, blue light presets, HDR, screen blackout, sleep and shut down in one panel.
  • Day, Night, Movie, Game and your own saved modes, one tap each.
  • Windows 10 and 11 with an external DDC/CI monitor. Pay once, try free for 7 days.

VYRON AgentOS

An automation system that takes over repetitive work. Agents go through the inbox, write the daily summary, take and verify backups and keep an eye on costs. You watch it all from one panel, and you can give commands from Telegram too.

  • Watchdogs: disk, errors, delays — you hear about it when something goes wrong.
  • Scheduled jobs: a summary every morning at 07:00, nightly backups, monthly reports.
  • Telegram: type "summarise today" and the job shows up on the panel.
  • Browser automation: fills in forms, collects data, takes screenshots.
  • Archiving: saves YouTube channels and websites with transcripts and images, searchable in plain language.
  • Legal digest: reads Türkiye's Official Gazette every morning and summarises the changes that affect your sector.
  • Backup checks: opens and tests backups automatically to make sure they aren't corrupt.
  • Set up for your business in 2 to 4 weeks. Your data stays with you.

Voice Chief (Sesli Şef)

Not a dictation tool: you hold a spoken conversation, in Turkish, with the AI agent in your terminal (Claude Code, ChatGPT, Codex). You speak, the agent answers out loud, and after a while you keep working without looking at the screen.

  • Most tools only handle dictation; here the answer comes back as speech too.
  • Audio never leaves the server: transcription runs locally.
  • You can also type instead of speaking.
  • Rests your eyes during long sessions without losing track of the work.

About

IronVision Tools

IronVision Tools is a software start-up based in Edirne, Türkiye. We build tools that take repetitive work off a business's hands: we set them up, the product runs on your side and your data stays with you. We're growing with support from the AWS startup programme.

We use our products ourselves first. Today more than ten small applications run inside AgentOS: watchdogs, a backup checker, the voice bridge, email and booking lines, the website production line and a semantic search engine. We don't recommend a tool to customers that doesn't work for us.

Where we're heading is clear: a business assistant that runs itself. Each of these small applications is gradually becoming a product of its own, and MoyMote is the first to reach the Microsoft Store. We're open to partnerships and support.
