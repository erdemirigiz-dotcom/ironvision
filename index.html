Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How is the base crypto profit calculated?

The system first takes your initial investment and converts it to USD using live market APIs if necessary. It then divides this amount by your buying price to determine your exact token holdings. Finally, it multiplies your holdings by the current selling price to find your gross profit.

2. What are crypto tax deductions and holding periods?

In many jurisdictions (such as the US, UK, and parts of the EU), your tax liability depends heavily on how long you hold the asset. Assets held for less than 1 year are typically subject to higher Short-Term Capital Gains brackets, while holding for over 1 year qualifies for reduced Long-Term rates.

3. How does the live currency conversion work?

If you input your investment in TRY, GBP, or EUR, our engine fetches the latest fiat exchange rates. It calculates the raw crypto mechanics in USD (as global crypto pairs operate primarily on USD/USDT), and translates the final net profit back to your local currency for real-world accuracy.
