Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How is the base crypto profit calculated?

The system converts your investment to USD (live rates when a non‑USD currency is chosen), divides by the buying price to find token amount, then multiplies by the current selling price for gross profit.

2. What are crypto tax deductions?

Short‑term (≤ 1 year) holdings are usually taxed at higher rates, while long‑term (> 1 year) holdings may qualify for reduced rates in many jurisdictions.

3. How does live currency conversion work?

If you enter TRY/GBP/EUR, we fetch live fiat rates, run the crypto math in USD, then convert the final profit back to your selected local currency.
