How a project runs

Five people-shaped steps, one machine jury

  1. 01

    Read the video

    We inventory your footage and pull the atmosphere — light, tempo, the one true colour. No stock, no invented imagery.

  2. 02

    Choose a stance

    An archetype is picked from the mood of your clip, not a menu. If it echoes a recent build, we reject it and start again.

  3. 03

    Write & build

    Copy in your languages, then a static, framework-free build with a real signature moment — never a lone fade-up.

  4. 04

    Face the jury

    Four independent critics plus an automated auditor score the site as if it were entering an award. It ships only when it passes.

  5. 05

    Hand off

    A 48-hour demo, three rounds of your taste, then the keys. Real photos and reviews take their place before launch.

What "award-grade" means here

Craft you can measure, not just admire

  • AA real signature moment
    One interaction born from your product — a flow meter, an orbit, a page that breathes with the scroll.
  • BPerformance as a constraint
    Static HTML/CSS/JS with locally-vendored GSAP. JS under 150 KB gzip, first load under 5 MB, Lighthouse ≥ 90.
  • CAccessible by contract
    WCAG AA contrast computed per pair, keyboard paths, reduced-motion honoured, screen-reader order intact.
  • DMultilingual from day one
    Copy written — not machine-dumped — in your markets, with correct dates, currency and cultural register.
