06 / FAQ

Before you ask a date

Do I pay for your travel?

For destination work, travel and accommodation are quoted transparently up front — no surprises after booking. Within your region, travel is folded into the day rate. You always see the full number before you commit.

How long until I get the photos?

A hand-picked sneak set arrives within 72 hours. The full, colour-graded gallery is delivered within four weeks of the shoot; rush delivery is available on request for an added fee.

Who owns the images, and how can I use them?

I retain copyright as the author, and you receive a clear written licence for your use — personal, print and web for couples; defined commercial usage or buy-out for brands. Nothing is published without your say-so.

Do you deliver the raw files?

Editing is part of the craft, so raw files aren't part of standard delivery. You receive high-resolution, finished files plus web-ready versions. If your workflow genuinely needs raws, we can discuss a separate archive licence.

What happens if we cancel or reschedule?

The date is held with a deposit. Reschedules are honoured subject to availability; if you cancel, the deposit covers the reserved date and any non-recoverable travel already booked. The full terms are written into your agreement — no fine print.

Where are you based, and how far will you go?

I work as an itinerant photographer with no fixed studio, which means most of the world is on the table. Tell me the location in your enquiry and I'll confirm feasibility and any travel window with the quote.
