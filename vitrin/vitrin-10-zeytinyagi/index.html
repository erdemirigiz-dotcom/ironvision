Good to know

Shipping, cold chain & the details

How is the oil shipped — is there a cold chain?
Olive oil is shipped at room temperature, not chilled — heat and light are the real enemies, not cool weather. Every bottle is dark glass, boxed away from light, and dispatched within [PLACEHOLDER] business days. Keep it in a cool, dark cupboard once it arrives.
What does the acidity figure mean?
Free acidity measures how gently the fruit was handled and pressed — lower is better. Extra virgin must sit under 0.8%; our lots run 0.19–0.28%. The exact figure is printed on each lot's dossier.
How long does it keep, and when should I use it?
Best within [PLACEHOLDER] months of the harvest date on the bottle — olive oil is a fresh-crop product, not a vintage to cellar. Once opened, use within a couple of months for the fullest flavour.
What if a bottle arrives damaged?
If anything arrives leaking or broken, send a photo within [PLACEHOLDER] days and we replace it, no return needed. Unopened bottles can be returned within [PLACEHOLDER] days.
Is it really from a single estate?
Yes — one grove, one hillside, pressed on its own. We never blend across estates or harvests, which is why each lot tastes a little different and carries its own dossier.
